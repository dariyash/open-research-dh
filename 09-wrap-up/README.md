# Course Wrap-up

## What you have learned
Across the course you moved through a complete open-research arc:
- understood **open research in the humanities** and why FAIR and CARE belong together;
- **documented** your project and put it under **version control** on GitHub;
- **found and assessed** open heritage data, and learned why the same entity hides under many names;
- used **AI as a checked, documented tool** to obtain and filter data;
- **cleaned, analysed, visualised, and interpreted** your data honestly;
- *(optionally)* **enriched** it by reconciling entities to Wikidata identifiers;
- **audited** your project for reproducibility with the TIER protocol;
- **published** your created dataset openly with a DOI and checked it against FAIR.

## What we did not cover
This is an introduction. Larger topics, advanced text/network analysis, linked-data modelling, long-term
preservation, and domain-specific ethics, are natural next steps. The recurring threads (authority control;
documenting every decision) will serve you in all of them.
